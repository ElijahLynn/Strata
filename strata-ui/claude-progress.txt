  is the ADR-0007 reference, but it's a DOM/UMD bundle that emits HTML; we render
  St.Label-only and must never markup-parse clipboard content). highlight(code)
  returns typed tokens that TILE the source exactly; Peek maps token type→colour
  and lays them down as Pango FOREGROUND attributes over UTF-8 byte ranges via
  clutter_text.set_attributes — NOT set_markup. isCode is gated on structural
  punctuation ({};, =>, def f(…) or ≥2 keywords + source symbols), so prose
  ("…with nothing special…") isn't colorized just for containing a keyword.

  intercepts before the focused card's St.Button activates-on-Space (which would
  copy). No card focused (focus in search) → Space falls through and types. While
  peeking, Space/Escape dismiss the Peek and all other keys are swallowed (so
  Escape closes the Peek, not the visor). _hideVisor() also closes any Peek.
